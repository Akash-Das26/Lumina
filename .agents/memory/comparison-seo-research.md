## Rule
Comparison pages should use public, current provider or official help pages for product claims, clearly label third-party references, and describe tradeoffs rather than inventing scores or weaknesses.

**Why:** Public research confirmed Monica's pricing page says free users have a daily usage limit; Perplexity publicly promotes clickable citations; OpenAI documents export. Other complaint evidence was inconsistent, so the site avoids unsupported claims.

**How to apply:** When adding or revising a competitor page, link to the evidence source, use broad pricing language unless verified from the provider, and make Lumina's advantages concrete (source cards, text-file context, exports) without claiming universal superiority.
